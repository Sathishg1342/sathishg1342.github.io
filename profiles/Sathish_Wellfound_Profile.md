# Wellfound Profile — Updated Content

Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) is a startup-focused job board, structurally different from Naukri/
LinkedIn/enterprise ATS systems: **your profile IS your application** — most startups use one-click apply with
no cover letter, and matching is driven by role/skills/preference fields rather than keyword search ranking.
Wellfound doesn't publish per-field character limits; constraints below are the ones actually confirmed via
Wellfound's own help documentation, flagged clearly from best-practice recommendations where no limit exists.

---

## Confirmed constraints / mechanics

- **Resume/CV upload** speeds up setup by auto-filling profile fields — verify parsed data afterward, same as
  other platforms.
- **"Years of Experience" is system-tracked** and gates eligibility — some roles have a minimum-YOE threshold
  you must meet to apply. Your 3+ years should clear early/mid-level startup roles without issue.
- **No cover letter in most flows** — your profile's Headline, About/Bio, and Skills sections do the work a
  cover letter would elsewhere. This makes those sections higher-leverage than on Naukri/LinkedIn.
- **No published character limits** on Headline, About, or Work Experience fields.
- Profiles are **private by default**, with candidate control over recruiter-outreach visibility.
- **Identity verification** step exists as part of profile completion (builds trust with startups).

## Practical notes

1. **Your profile replaces the cover letter here** — the About/Bio section is doing work that Google/Microsoft/
   Amazon push into a separate cover-letter field. Keep it tight but don't under-write it like you might for
   Naukri's 1,000-char cap; there's no such constraint on Wellfound.
2. **Wellfound skews toward early-stage startups** — your solo-build track record (AiroStrip, Appointment Mitra,
   the chatbot widget) is a stronger signal here than at Google/Microsoft/Amazon, where team-scale delivery
   often matters more. Lead with it.
3. **Be honest about compensation and stage preferences** — unlike Naukri where you might anchor slightly high
   to stay visible in search, Wellfound startups see your stated expectations directly before reaching out, so
   misaligned numbers waste both sides' time rather than getting filtered by an algorithm.
