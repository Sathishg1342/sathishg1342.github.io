# Google Careers Profile — Updated Content

Google's application system is much simpler than Naukri/LinkedIn — there's no detailed key-skills tagging or
project sections. Most of the evaluation weight sits on your **resume itself** (parsed automatically) plus a few
structured fields. Fill these in on careers.google.com under "My Candidate Profile."

**Important constraints to know before applying:**
- Resume file must be **under 2 MB** (your Sathish_Resume_ATS.pdf is well under this — fine to use as-is)
- You can apply to **up to 3 jobs within a rolling 30-day window** — choose roles deliberately, don't spray-apply
- If rejected, you must **wait 90 days** before reapplying to the same role, and reviewers look for **12-18 months
  of added experience** on reapplication for technical roles — so don't reapply to the same posting quickly
- Once submitted, an application **cannot be edited** (including work authorization details) — review carefully
  before hitting submit each time

## Before you apply — practical notes

1. **Pick your 3 applications for this 30-day window deliberately.** Since Google caps applications at 3 per
   rolling 30 days, don't burn a slot on a role that's a weak fit — match to postings where your RAG/agentic AI
   and full-stack background is genuinely relevant (Search, Cloud AI, internal tools, healthcare-adjacent teams).
2. **A referral matters more at Google than almost anywhere else** — if you know anyone at Google, ask for a
   referral before cold-applying; it materially changes recruiter response rates.
3. Given the 90-day reapply cooldown and the "12-18 months more experience" expectation for technical
   reapplications, don't treat Google applications as a fast-iteration channel — this is a slower, more
   deliberate track than Naukri/LinkedIn outreach, not a parallel fast option for your near-term job search.
